PRIVACY POLICY

Our Commitment to Your Privacy and Data Security

1. The Information We Gather

To provide you with seamless property maintenance solutions, we collect essential contact details when you voluntarily reach out to us. This may include your Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and relevant Business Information. We gather this data exclusively to facilitate clear communication, deliver our services, and provide exceptional support.

2. Putting Your Information to Work

Your data is utilized strictly for legitimate, operational business purposes. We use this information to:

  • Communicate regarding ongoing or future services
  • Provide real-time updates on your projects
  • Confirm and manage appointments
  • Deliver prompt customer support and troubleshoot issues

3. Text Messaging & 10DLC Compliance

By providing your phone number, you authorize NRH Solutions to send you SMS messages regarding your requested services, project updates, and account notifications. Please note:

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with our team.
  • Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

You remain in total control. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP if you need assistance.

4. Our Strict Zero-Sharing Policy

Your trust is our foundation. We absolutely do not sell, rent, or share your personal data—especially your phone number—with third-party marketers or data brokers. Your details are kept securely in-house to serve you better.

5. Trusted Service Partners

Occasionally, we utilize secure third-party platforms (such as secure SMS gateways) to route our communications to you. These carefully vetted providers are legally bound to maintain the highest levels of confidentiality and are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.

6. Safeguarding Your Data

We have implemented robust digital and physical security protocols to shield your personal information from unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or misuse. Your privacy is treated with the utmost respect.

7. Your Consent to Connect

When you submit your contact details via our website, digital forms, or direct correspondence, you are providing your express consent for NRH Solutions to contact you via SMS, phone, or email to fulfill your service requests.

8. Your Right to Step Away

You can revoke your consent for SMS communications instantly. Simply reply STOP to any text message you receive from us, and you will be immediately removed from our SMS registry.

9. Policy Evolution

As industry regulations and operational standards evolve, we may update this policy. Any changes will be published directly on this page to ensure you are always informed.

Mobile Data Disclaimer: No mobile information will be shared with any third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with or obtained by any third parties.

If you wish to be removed from receiving future communications, you can opt out at any time by texting STOP to our number or by contacting us at contact@nrhsolutions.com.
